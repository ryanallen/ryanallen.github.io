Every drawing in the Voynich manuscript that is not a plant: the zodiac signs, the creatures, the castle, the suns, moons and stars, the wheels, the women, the jars and the pools, grouped by what they show and then by how surely each can be named. Secure: what is drawn is plain and nothing else fits. Likely: the features fit one reading better than the rest. Possible: a reading fits some features, or two fit as well. Unknown: nothing names it. Open a card for the reason, every published identification and who made it, and the words written on or beside it.
