Does any language have the Voynich's habits? No.

Nothing on this page cracks the code. No row is a translation, and the closest rows are fakes.
How to read the charts. Each dot is one text. Its distance from the left edge is how different its habits are from the Voynich's. The green band is where the Voynich lands against its own pages. A language would need a dot in the green band before it's worth trying to read the Voynich in it, and even then it would only be a lead. No language gets there.

Do the pages split into groups?
