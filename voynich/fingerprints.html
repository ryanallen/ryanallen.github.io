No tested language comes close to the Voynich text. We compared its letters, words, word order, and predictability with eight languages a writer in the Alps around 1400 might have known. We also tested abbreviated forms, missing vowels, syllable writing, a cipher, and text made by rules. A shorter bar means a closer match. The bars start near the best score so you can see the gaps. These results use 187 working pages; the test pages are saved for a final check.
