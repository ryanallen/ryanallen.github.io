Each Voynich page here has a plant drawn in some way like one in Penn's Erbario (Oversize LJS 419), a herbal written in northern Italy in the 1400s. Beside each pair: what agrees, what does not, and the Erbario's own name for its plant and what its note says, put into English. A partial likeness is one feature shared, most often a way of drawing the root; it is a lead, not an identification.
