Seven Voynich plants look partly like plants in another 1400s herbal. Compare them with the Erbario, a manuscript from northern Italy held by Penn Libraries. Each pair shows what looks alike, what differs, the Erbario plant's name, and its note in English. A shared feature, often the way a root is drawn, is a lead. It does not identify the Voynich plant.
