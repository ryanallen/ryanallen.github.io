Every proposed reading of the Voynich text, put to one test. Each candidate is written as three parts kept apart: P, the language or kind of text it claims; E, how that text becomes Voynich signs; and T, anything further done to it. Each run is written down, with its question and what it predicts, before it is run, and runs only on the pages held for development; the 40 final pages stay closed until a candidate is frozen. A run's text is measured the way the sign grid measures the Voynich. A measure is green when it lies within the Voynich's own spread, the gap between its two "languages" (Currier A and B), and red when it does not. A reading passes only if more than 80% of the repeated words read, above what the same method reads from the Voynich text shuffled, and every required measure is green.
