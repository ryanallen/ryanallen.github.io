How a solution can pass

It must turn its claimed language or system into Voynich letters, explain the text's line and word patterns, read more than 80% of repeated words, and beat the same method on shuffled text. We use working pages for development and save 40 pages for a final test. Green scores fall within the range between the manuscript's two main text groups, Currier A and B.

In the scorecards, line patterns are changes at the start or end of a handwritten line; near repeats are neighboring words that differ by one letter; and new words are words a proposed system creates that the manuscript never uses.
