The Voynich signs grouped by how they behave, as Alice Kober and Michael Ventris grouped the signs of Linear B before anyone could read it, with no sound values. Each column holds signs that stand in like places and among like neighbours; the bar under each sign shows how often it opens a word, stands inside one, ends one, or is a word alone. Counted on the 187 development pages of the Zandbergen–Landini transliteration in EVA; the pictures are the signs as the tracing site keeps them.
