A program that reads the Voynich script from a photograph of a page and writes it in EVA, the alphabet most transcriptions use. It learned the letters from every line the tracing has keyed, and is tested here on twenty pages it never saw, drawn across all five scribes. It finds the lines itself, cuts them out and reads them. Each line below shows the scan, what the reader read, and the key (the tracing's own letters). Red marks a letter the reader got wrong, and · a letter it left out.
