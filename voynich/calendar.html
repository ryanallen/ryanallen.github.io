These pages count zodiac degrees, not calendar days. Each sign is ringed by women holding stars, usually with a word beside each one. See them in order, clockwise from the top; click a word to find it elsewhere. Below are the bathing pools grouped by water color and three 1400s German zodiacs beside the Voynich signs. Pages marked final are saved for the final test.
What was found
  • About one woman per degree. Most signs have 30 women. Aries and Taurus each have two pages of 15; Pisces has 29. Months with 31 days still have 30 positions.
  • Tubs appear only by Pisces, Aries, and Taurus. These signs covered the spring bathing season in 1400s calendars, with herbal baths in May.
  • Words beside the women do not repeat at the same position from sign to sign, as a day-by-day calendar's would.
  • Most bathing pools have green water (18 of 24), with no pattern by page.
  • The closest pictured zodiac: St. Gallen Cod. Sang. 827 (Lake Constance, 1425–1428) alone shares the crossbowman Sagittarius and the lizard-like Scorpio.
