The manuscript writes single words, labels, beside its stars, zodiac women, jars and the roots and leaves of the pharmaceutical pages. Here every label stands with the thing it was found beside, its picture from Yale's scan, and its reading in EVA (click a word to find it everywhere else). Below the pictures: the tests of whether a label names what it stands beside, the words that gather by topic, and every note in the Latin alphabet, the month names among them. Pages marked final are held out for the last test and counted in nothing here.
