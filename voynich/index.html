How it works

No one has read the Voynich manuscript. A computer has traced its letters, pixel by pixel, but it makes mistakes. You check them, one letter at a time.

  • The letter to check is in bright color; the rest of the word is faint. Paint over its ink with the brush, and erase what isn't its ink. Hold H (or press Hide) to see the page underneath. [ and ] make the brush smaller and bigger. Esc goes back to the page, and from a page to the list of pages.
  • If it is a different letter, pick it under "It's". Examples of each letter from other pages are shown, and so is every version of this letter: the computer's and other people's.
  • When the letter is right, press the button and the next one comes. Your work saves as you go; there is nothing to send.
  • When two people's versions of a letter agree, it is verified. Missing words can be added from a page: "+ Add a missing word".
  • You stay anonymous unless you want credit. "Get credited" at the top takes a name or handle for the credits when the work is released.

Get credited

Everyone who helps is credited when the glyph map, the OCR and the font are released. Leave the name or handle you want listed, or leave it empty to stay anonymous. It is only shown in the credits, never beside your tracings.
